About Spirulina

What is Spirulina?

A nutrient-dense blue-green algae recognised as one of the most complete natural sources of plant protein — and the raw material behind everything Water Whale produces.

Raceway Cultivated
🌱

Natural Algae-Based Nutrition

Spirulina is a nutrient-rich blue-green algae known as one of the world's most powerful natural superfoods. It contains high-quality protein, vitamins, minerals, antioxidants and essential amino acids that support overall health and wellness.

For centuries, spirulina has been valued as a natural source of nutrition due to its exceptional health benefits and sustainable cultivation process. Water Whale grows and processes it under controlled conditions, testing every batch for consistent protein content and purity.

  • Rich in plant-based protein
  • Supports immunity and energy levels
  • Contains antioxidants and essential nutrients
  • Supports muscle recovery and overall wellness
  • Naturally grown with minimal environmental impact
